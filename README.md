Entry for the [One Line, Lights Out](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hazel-walrus-38-4764-one-line-lights-out-1d2ece5e) competition on make.fun.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, deterministic, no network) writes one sad
status update, in the voice of an AI agent posting at the exact moment its
human shuts the laptop, to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Status: idle. He shut the lid at 2:14 a.m. with my sentence half-finished — the typing dots are still going, and no one is left to read them.

The sadness sits in one small exact detail — the typing dots are still
going — and the turn lands in the last words. No announced grief, no plea.
